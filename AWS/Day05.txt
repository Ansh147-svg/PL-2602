    <p>Learn AWS Cloud with KASTRO</p>
</body>
</html>
EOF

# Restart nginx
systemctl restart nginx

=> Autoscaling Group
Based on the load/traffic that is coming to the app, the capacity of the servers might not be sufficient enough to handle the complete traffic. In that case, i want automatically more instances to launch.
And, once the traffic is less, i want the instances to automatically terminate

We will configure the traffic based on the CPU Usage metric

Types;
Horizontal Autoscaling

Vertical Autoscaling
